Support

Something wrong, or an idea to share? Write to us and we'll get back to you soon:

Email: advikerem@gmail.com

Adding your phone model and the level number helps us help you faster.

Where is my progress saved?

Your levels, coins and decorations are saved on your phone. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

My "Remove Ads" purchase is missing.

Go to Settings → Restore purchases. If you bought it with the same Apple ID, it comes back.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Without internet no ads are shown, so rewards that come from watching an ad are not available at that moment.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The game is available in 26 languages.

I don't want personalized ads.

On your iPhone go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn it off for Sleigh Jam. In Europe and some US states you can also use the Ad Choices button in the game's Settings. You will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to you.

I want my data deleted.

Write to us and include the player ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen. Details are in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

2. Hangi bilgileri, neden ve hangi hukuki dayanakla işliyoruz

a) Telefonunda kalan bilgiler

Oyun ilerlemen (bölüm, altın, güçlendirmeler, süsler), ayarların (ses, müzik, titreşim, bildirimler, dil), reklam onayı tercihin, satın alma kayıtların ve oyunun ilk açılışta oluşturduğu rastgele oyuncu kimliği (16 karakter; adınla ya da cihazınla ilişkili değildir) telefonunda saklanır. Bunlar kendi sunucumuza gönderilmez. Amaç: oyunu sana sunmak. Dayanak: sözleşmenin ifası (GDPR m. 6/1-b; KVKK m. 5/2-c).

b) Oyun istatistikleri (Google Firebase Analytics)

Oyun olayları (ör. bölüm başladı / kazanıldı / kaybedildi, süre, dokunma sayısı, güçlendirme ve "devam et" kullanımı, açılan pencereler, reklam ve satın alma olayları), oyun durumun (seviye, altın aralığı, dil, ses/müzik ayarı, satın alma sayısı), oyuncu kimliği, Firebase'in uygulama örneği kimliği, cihaz bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, saat dilimi) ve IP adresinden çıkarılan yaklaşık konum (ülke/şehir) toplanır. Amaç: zor ya da sıkıcı bölümleri bulmak, oyunu dengelemek ve hataları düzeltmek. Dayanak: Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de onayın (onay vermezsen kimlik içermeyen, çerezsiz sinyaller dışında istatistik gönderilmez); diğer ülkelerde oyunu geliştirmedeki meşru menfaatimiz (GDPR m. 6/1-f; KVKK m. 5/2-f). İtiraz etmek için bize yazabilirsin.

c) Çökme raporları (Firebase Crashlytics)

Oyun çökerse hata kaydı, cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi, uygulama sürümü, o anki ekran / bölüm ve oyuncu kimliği gönderilir. Amaç: oyunu kararlı ve güvenli tutmak. Dayanak: meşru menfaat.

d) Uzaktan ayarlar (Firebase Remote Config)

Oyun, güncelleme gerektirmeden zorluk ve fiyat gibi ayarları almak için Firebase'e bir kurulum kimliğiyle bağlanır. Dayanak: sözleşmenin ifası ve meşru menfaat.

Oyun ücretsizdir ve reklam gösterir. Reklamları Google AdMob ve AdMob üzerinden teklif veren reklam ortakları sunar. Reklamı göstermek, görüntülendiğini ölçmek, aynı reklamın çok sık çıkmasını ve sahtekârlığı önlemek için şunlar işlenebilir: cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi, dil, IP adresi ve yaklaşık konum, reklam etkileşimleri, uygulama içi reklam kimliği (IDFV) ve yalnızca iPhone'da "İzleme" iznini verirsen reklam tanımlayıcısı (IDFA). Oyunda en fazla "PG" (genel izleyici, aile gözetimi) içerik düzeyindeki reklamlar gösterilir. Dayanak: kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar ve cihazda bilgi saklama için onayın (Avrupa'da Google'ın onay formu (Google User Messaging Platform; seçimin telefonunda saklanır), tüm dünyada Apple'ın İzleme izni); diğer durumlarda ücretsiz oyunu finanse etmedeki meşru menfaatimiz. Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

In short: There is no account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Your progress stays on your phone. To improve the game we collect game statistics that don't identify you, plus crash reports. Ads come from Google AdMob and are personalized only if you allow it. You can change your choices any time in the game's Settings and in your iPhone settings.

1. Who is responsible

Sleigh Jam is published by the independent developer Kerem Can Demirtaş (Istanbul, Türkiye), the data controller for the information in this policy ("we"). Contact: advikerem@gmail.com

2. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

a) Information that stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, decorations), settings (sound, music, vibration, notifications, language), your ad consent choice, purchase records and a random player ID the game creates on first launch (16 characters, not linked to your name or device) are stored on your phone. They are not sent to a server of ours. Purpose: to provide the game. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

b) Game statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

We collect game events (for example a level started / won / lost, play time, number of taps, use of boosters and continues, windows opened, ad and purchase events), your game state (level, coin range, language, sound/music setting, number of purchases), the player ID, Firebase's app instance ID, device information (model, operating system, language, time zone) and the approximate location derived from the IP address (country/city). Purpose: to find levels that are too hard or boring, balance the game and fix bugs. Legal basis: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland your consent (without it, no statistics are sent apart from cookieless signals without identifiers); elsewhere our legitimate interest in improving the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object by writing to us.

c) Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, the error log, device model, operating system, app version, the screen / level at that moment and the player ID are sent. Purpose: keeping the game stable and secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

d) Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The game connects to Firebase with an installation ID to receive settings such as difficulty and prices without an app update. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legitimate interest.

e) Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads, served by Google AdMob and the ad partners that bid through AdMob. To show ads, measure that they were seen, limit how often the same ad appears and prevent fraud, these may be processed: device model, operating system, language, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions, the in-app vendor identifier (IDFV) and, only if you allow "Tracking" on your iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA). Ads are personalized only with your permission; otherwise you see non-personalized or limited ads. Ads are limited to the "PG" (general audiences, parental guidance) content rating. Legal basis: your consent for personalized ads and for storing information on your device (Google's consent form in Europe (Google User Messaging Platform; your choice is stored on your phone), Apple's Tracking permission everywhere); otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free game. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

f) In-app purchases (Apple, Unity IAP)

Apple processes payments; we never see your card details. The game talks to the App Store through Unity Technologies' purchasing library (Unity IAP), which may send anonymous usage and diagnostic data to Unity. The game only receives the product and the transaction number. The transaction number is kept on your phone so a purchase is never delivered twice, and the purchase event (product, price, currency) is also recorded in the statistics. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

g) Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your phone; no data is sent to a server for them. Their timing is worked out on your phone from the hour you usually play. They appear only if you allow notifications in iOS, and you can turn them off in Settings → Notifications.

h) Support messages

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

i) Game engine (Unity)

The game is made with Unity. Unity Technologies may receive anonymous hardware statistics (such as device model, operating system and graphics chip) to improve the engine: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

3. Automated decisions

The game adjusts level difficulty on your phone based on your results (for example, it gets a little easier after many losses). This has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. Who receives information

Only the service providers above: Google (Firebase, AdMob and AdMob's ad partners), Apple and Unity. We do not sell your data for money. Some US state laws treat sharing for personalized ads as a "sale" or "sharing"; see section 7 to opt out.

5. International transfers

These services run servers in the United States and other countries, so your information is processed outside your country. For transfers from Europe, Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Transfers from Türkiye follow Article 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

6. How long we keep it

  • Information on your phone: until you delete the app.
  • Game statistics: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically by Google.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ad data: for Google's own retention periods.
  • Support emails: up to 12 months after the matter is closed.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: in Europe, change or withdraw your consent with the Ad Choices button in the game's Settings. In the US states with privacy laws, the same button lets you opt out of the sale / sharing of your personal information.
  • Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also reset your advertising identifier there.
  • Notifications: the game's Settings → Notifications, or iPhone Settings → Notifications.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability: write to us and include the player ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen. Because the ID is random, it is the only way we can find your data. We answer within 30 days and delete statistics and crash records with Google's tools. Deleting the app removes everything on your phone.
  • United States (California and other states): you have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale/sharing, and you won't be treated differently for using these rights.
  • Brazil (LGPD) and Türkiye (KVKK Art. 11): the same rights can be used by writing to us.
  • Complaints: you can contact your local data protection authority in the EU, the ICO in the UK, the KVKK Board in Türkiye, the ANPD in Brazil or the CPPA in California.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16), please play with a parent. If a child's data has reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

There are no accounts or passwords. Information sent to the service providers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new text and date on this page, and announce important changes in the game.
